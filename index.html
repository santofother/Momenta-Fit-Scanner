Ready to scan
Tap here if scanning stops working
  1. Connect the scanner in Socket Mobile Open the Socket Mobile app and make sure your scanner is paired. If it won't connect, go to Settings > Bluetooth and check that the scanner appears there first.
  2. Switch to Socket Mobile Keyboard Tap the scan field, then press the globe icon on the keyboard and cycle until you see "Socket Mobile Keyboard." When correct, "Scanner Connected" will appear on the keyboard screen.
  3. Tap into the scan field before scanning The scanner only registers barcodes when the scan input is active. Always tap the scan area first.
  4. Scan and confirm A successful scan will flash the screen green and display the client's name. If nothing happens, repeat steps 1–3.
